RUA: Your Learning Workflow

Every word goes through three stages. Every stage is about YOU.

R

Recognise

Meaning. Form. Pronunciation. A core metaphor in your native language that helps connect the word to what you already know.

U

Understand

Collocation. Contexts. Connotation. Every example sentence uses your hobby. Basketball. Diving. Marketing. Whatever you love.

A

Apply

Cross-linguistic mapping. Why your L1 makes this word tricky. Practice exercises. A thesaurus at your level — synonyms you can actually use.
